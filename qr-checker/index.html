    What to do before you pay or log in
      Read the address first

      The camera shows the web address before it opens. A parking payment on “parking-pago.top” or a bank login on a short link is the scam, however official the page looks.

      Stickers over stickers

      Fake QR codes get stuck on top of real ones on parking meters, chargers, bikes and posters. Pay through the official app or at the machine itself, and run your finger over the code to feel for a sticker.

      You never scan to get paid

      Receiving money never needs a QR code. Neither does a real login request by email, and scanning a code with WhatsApp or Telegram links your account to someone else’s computer.
