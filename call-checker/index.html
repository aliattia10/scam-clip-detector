Describe what the caller said: your bank’s “fraud department”, a transfer to a “safe account”, the police, Microsoft or your internet provider, an app to install, a courier coming for your card, or a crying relative who needs money now. In one second you get a red-flag score and what to do next.

    What to do now
      There is no “safe account”

      No bank, police force or regulator will ever ask you to move your money to protect it. Anyone who says so on the phone is the thief.

      The number on screen can be faked

      Caller ID spoofing lets scammers show your bank’s real number. Hang up and call the number on your card yourself.

      Voices can be cloned

      A few seconds of audio is enough to copy a relative’s voice. Agree a family code word and call back on their usual number.
