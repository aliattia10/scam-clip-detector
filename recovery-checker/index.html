    What to do now
      Nobody honest needs your seed

      Recovery that asks for a seed phrase, private key or “wallet sync” is theft. Legitimate help never needs those.

      Upfront fee = walk away

      Gas fees, unlock fees, tax clearance or “agent retainers” paid in crypto before any work are the classic second hit.

      Police don’t DM recoveries

      FBI, Interpol, CNMV or “cyber units” will not cold-message you on Telegram to recover coins for a USDT fee.
