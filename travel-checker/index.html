    What to do before you pay
      Real app, fake message

      Criminals break into hotels’ accounts and write to guests through the real booking chat. If a message asks you to “re-confirm” your card on a link, close it and check the reservation in the app yourself.

      Pay inside the platform

      Booking sites only protect you when you pay and talk through them. A host who offers a discount for paying by transfer, Bizum or WhatsApp is taking you outside that protection on purpose.

      Support does not DM you

      Airline “agents” who answer your complaint on X or Instagram, or numbers in sponsored search ads, are a classic trap. Use only the contact page of the airline’s own website or app.
