    What to do now
      Getting paid takes zero steps

      No real platform asks a seller to enter card details, accept a Bizum request or scan a QR code to receive money.

      Stay inside the app

      Wallapop, Vinted and Marketplace only protect chats and payments made in the app. “Add me on WhatsApp” is how scammers get you out of that protection.

      An email is not money

      A “payment received” email, SMS or screenshot proves nothing. Check your balance in the app or your bank yourself before you ship.
