    What to do before you give
      Pay the charity, never a person

      Real charities take donations on their own website, by card, by transfer to an account in the charity’s name, or in Spain through Bizum’s “Donate” option with the NGO’s 5-digit code. A Bizum to “Laura, coordinator” is a gift to Laura.

      Check it is real in two minutes

      Type the charity’s address yourself, look for its full legal name and tax number (CIF in Spain), and search it in the official register of charities, associations or foundations. No name, no number, no website: no donation.

      Disasters bring out the fakes

      After a flood, a fire, a war or a viral sick-child story, fake appeals appear within hours. Give to organisations already working there, through their own channels, not through a forwarded message, a comment or a new account.
