    What to do now
      Real jobs do not charge you to start

      Training fees, “equipment deposits”, software licences paid by the candidate — those are classic advance-fee scams, not onboarding.

      Weird pay channels are a stop sign

      Crypto, gift cards, wire to a stranger, or “cash this cheque and send the rest back” are how money-mule and overpayment scams work.

      Verify the company, not the logo

      Lookalike domains, free-mail HR addresses and “hired with no interview” pitches steal IDs and bank details. Call the real company switchboard.
