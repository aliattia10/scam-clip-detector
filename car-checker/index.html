    What to do before you pay or hand over the keys
      No viewing, no deal

      Fake car ads use real photos copied from dealers abroad. If the seller cannot meet, will not do a video call from next to the car, or says it is in another country and a transport or escrow company will bring it, the car usually does not exist.

      Check the car, not the story

      In Spain anyone can get the DGT vehicle report with the plate: owner changes, ITV, mileage records, embargos and whether it can be transferred. The VIN on the car must match the ficha técnica and the permiso de circulación.

      Selling? Only real money counts

      A screenshot, an email “from the bank” or a pending transfer is not money. Never refund an overpayment, never share an SMS code and never hand over the car and its papers until the full amount is in your own banking app.
