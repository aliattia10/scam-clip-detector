    What to do right now
      Real warnings have no phone number

      Windows Security, macOS and real antivirus apps show alerts inside the program, never a number to call. A browser page that beeps, talks or will not close is just a web page. Close the browser and it is gone.

      Microsoft does not call you

      Microsoft, Apple, Amazon and your internet provider do not watch your computer and do not phone you about errors, hacked IPs or expired licences. An unexpected technical call is the scam.

      Remote access plus your bank equals theft

      Once they control your screen they open your online banking, fake a refund with an extra zero and ask you to send back the difference in gift cards or crypto. Never open your bank while anyone is connected.
