    What to do before you pay or send documents
      Appointments are free

      A cita previa for fingerprints, the TIE, the NIE certificate or most other procedures is booked for free on the official sede electrónica of the administration. Bots and resellers grab slots and sell them; paying them is risky and the appointment may not even be in your name.

      Fees go to the State, not to a person

      Official fees, like the tasa modelo 790 código 012 for police procedures, are small, fixed and paid with an official form at a bank or online. Nobody from the administration asks for a Bizum, a transfer to a personal account, a gift card or crypto.

      Nobody can guarantee a decision

      A real lawyer or gestor can charge for their own work, but they cannot promise approval, and they have a collegiate number you can check. Bought job contracts, fake padrón registrations or fake relationships can get your application refused and lead to criminal charges.
