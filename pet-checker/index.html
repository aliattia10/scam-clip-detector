    What to do before you pay
      No visit, no deal

      Most pet scams have no animal at all: the photos are copied from real breeders. A seller who cannot show the puppy with its mother, live or on a video call where you choose what they film, does not have it.

      The fees never stop

      After the first payment comes the crate, the insurance, the vaccine, the quarantine, the customs and the “refundable” deposit. Each one is a new story to get more money. Real breeders do not run a courier company that charges you by the stage.

      Ask for the chip and the register

      In Spain dogs, cats and ferrets must be microchipped and registered before they change hands, and breeders must be registered. Ask for the microchip number and the breeder registration number before you pay, and check them. A scammer will have an excuse.
