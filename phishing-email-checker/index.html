    What to do now
      Check the domain, not the logo

      Banks, Correos and Amazon never email from random .xyz / free-mail lookalikes. Hover every link before you tap.

      Urgent pay = stop

      Bizum, crypto, gift cards or “pay this invoice in 1 hour or we sue” is how phishing drains accounts before you verify.

      Never log in from the email

      Open the bank or shop by typing the real URL yourself. Attachment .html / .exe “invoices” are malware droppers.
