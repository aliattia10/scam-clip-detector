    What to do before you pay
      Sold out is the bait

      Ticket scammers wait for the big sell-outs. The more desperate buyers are, the less they check.

      No buyer protection, no deal

      Bizum, bank transfer, PayPal friends and family and crypto cannot be reversed. That is why scammers ask for them.

      A screenshot is not a ticket

      The same QR code or PDF can be sold to twenty people. Only the first one through the gate gets in.
