Is that online shop real?

Describe the shop: an ad on Instagram, Facebook or TikTok, a brand at 70% off, a closing-down sale with a countdown, a web address you have never seen, and a checkout that suddenly wants Bizum or a transfer. In one second you get a red-flag score and what to check before you pay.

    What to check before you pay
      The ad is the shop window

      Fake shops pay for ads on Instagram, Facebook and TikTok. An ad being approved says nothing about whether the shop is real.

      Card, not transfer

      Card payments and PayPal for goods and services let you dispute a charge. Bizum and bank transfers usually do not.

      Closing down forever

      Countdown timers and “last 3 left” banners on fake shops often reset when you reload the page. Real clearance sales do not need a stopwatch.
