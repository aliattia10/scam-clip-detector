    What to do now
      Guaranteed returns do not exist

      Real investments go up and down. Anyone promising fixed daily or monthly profit is describing a fraud.

      Celebrities did not recommend it

      Ads with famous faces and fake news articles are deepfakes. The real people have nothing to do with the platform.

      Paying to withdraw means it is gone

      If you must pay a tax or fee before you can take your money out, the balance is fake. Do not pay it.
