    What to do before you sign or pay
      Never pay to get a loan

      Real lenders take their costs out of the loan or add them to your instalments, after you sign a contract. Any “insurance”, “notary fee”, “opening commission” or “deposit” you must pay before the money arrives is the scam itself.

      Look the lender up yourself

      Find the company’s full legal name and tax number, type its website yourself, and search it in the official register of banks and credit companies (in Spain, the Banco de España register). A first name on WhatsApp is not a lender.

      Your account is not for hire

      If a “lender” sends money to your account and asks you to forward part of it, you are being used to move stolen money. That can freeze your account and get you charged. Never pass on money you did not expect.
