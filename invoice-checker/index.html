    What to check before you pay
      The account change is the whole trick

      Criminals get into a supplier’s mailbox, or register an address one letter off, wait for a real invoice and send it again with their own IBAN. Everything else in the email is genuine, which is why it works.

      Urgent and secret means call

      CEO fraud relies on “I’m in a meeting, can’t talk, don’t tell anyone”. A thirty-second call to the boss’s usual number ends it. Two people should approve any new or urgent payment.

      Official-looking is not official

      Domain “registrations”, trademark “publications”, business directories and GDPR “certificates” arrive with a payment slip that looks like a bill. The small print says it is an offer. Public bodies do not invoice like this.
