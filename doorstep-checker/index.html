    What to do before you open or pay
      Real visits are announced

      Regulated gas inspections and meter work are announced in advance by the distributor, by letter or on your bill, with a date and a way to check it. Nobody needs to “pass by today” without notice, and you can always say no and book it yourself.

      Nobody sells at the door for the gas company

      The scam is not the inspection, it is the sale: a hose, a regulator or a gas detector at five times the price, paid on the spot, with a threat to cut your supply or fine you. No law forces you to buy a part from someone who knocks on your door.

      Your bill and your IBAN stay inside

      An energy seller who asks to see your bill, photographs the CUPS code or gets you to sign on a tablet can switch your contract without you noticing. If you signed at home, Spanish law gives you 14 days to withdraw from a contract made outside a shop.

Gas, electricity and water companies, councils, locksmiths and builders appear only because scammers impersonate them; they are not accused of anything. Not legal or safety advice. If you smell gas, leave the building and call 112. If a fake technician is at the door now, do not open and call 091 (Policía Nacional) or 062 (Guardia Civil). If you already paid or signed, call your bank, keep the bill and any leaflet, file a complaint with your local consumer office (OMIC) and report it to the police.
