    What to do now
      Nobody can guarantee market returns

      Forex, crypto and stocks move. A “95% win rate” or “2% a day guaranteed” with no audited track record is marketing, not evidence.

      Paying for signals is rarely the product

      Many rooms make money from VIP fees, affiliate brokers and deposit funnels — not from your P&L. Ask who gets paid when you join.

      Screenshots are not proof

      PnL cards and “my student made 10x” posts are easy to fake or cherry-pick. Demand independent statements, not gallery shots.
