    What to do now
      No viewing, no money

      A deposit, “reservation fee” or first month paid before you have seen the place is the core of almost every rental scam.

      Keys never come by courier

      “I am abroad, I will send the keys by DHL / Airbnb after you pay” is the oldest script there is.

      Booking sites don’t take bank transfers by email

      Airbnb, Booking and Idealista never ask you to pay by transfer, Bizum, Western Union or crypto from a link in an email.
