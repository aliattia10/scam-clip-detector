    What to do now
      Couriers do not text for small fees

      A “1.99 to release your parcel” link is the most common smishing text in Europe. The tiny amount is only there to get your card number.

      Fines and refunds live in official apps

      Traffic fines, tolls and tax refunds are checked in the official app or website you type yourself, never through a link in a text.

      Call the old number first

      “Hi Mum, I lost my phone, this is my new number” followed by an urgent payment is a script. Call your child on the number you already have.
