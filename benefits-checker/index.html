    What to check before you click
      Refunds arrive on their own

      Hacienda, HMRC and other tax agencies pay refunds automatically to the account you already gave them. A message with a link to “claim” a refund exists to take your card or bank details.

      Paying to get paid is the scam

      No real grant, subsidy, voucher or benefit is released after a fee, commission or “tax” paid by Bizum, card, transfer or crypto. Public money costs nothing to receive.

      Your Cl@ve is your identity

      With your Cl@ve PIN or digital certificate someone can change the account where you are paid, file in your name or take out loans. No agent, gestor or “official” needs it from you by message.
