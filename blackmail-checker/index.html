    What to do instead of paying
      There is no video

      The “I hacked your camera” emails are sent to millions of addresses at once. The password they quote comes from an old data leak of some website, not from your device. Change it wherever you still use it and delete the email.

      Police do not email you to pay

      Europol, the Policía Nacional, the Guardia Civil and courts do not send accusations by email from Gmail or Outlook, and never ask you to “justify yourself” by reply or pay a fine to close a case. If you are worried, call 091 or 062 yourself.

      If it is real, paying never ends it

      If someone really has photos or a recording of you, paying only proves you will pay again. Stop replying, keep screenshots, report the account and go to the police. Adults can use StopNCII.org to help block the images on big platforms; under 18s can use Take It Down and should tell an adult they trust.
