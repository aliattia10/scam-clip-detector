    What to do before you pay, forward or open anything
      Overpayment is the whole trick

      The client “pays” more than the invoice by cheque, a transfer screenshot or a stolen card and asks you to send the difference to a designer, a supplier or back to them. Days later the payment bounces or is reversed, and the money you sent is gone.

      Clients pay you, not the other way

      A real client never asks you to buy a software license, a laptop, a training course, an “account upgrade” or an escrow fee from a vendor they choose before the work starts, even if they promise to reimburse it.

      Never be the pipe for someone else’s money

      “Receive payments from our customers and forward them minus 10%” is not a job, it is money laundering, and the person whose account was used is the one the police find. Never lend your bank, PayPal, Bizum or crypto account.
