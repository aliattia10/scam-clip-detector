    ✅ What to do now
      🔑 Nobody legit asks for your seed phrase

      Not support, not an airdrop, not a “wallet sync”. Anyone with those 12/24 words owns your wallet.

      🎁 A giveaway never needs you to send first

      “Send 1, get 2 back” is the oldest trick in crypto. The only thing that gets doubled is their wallet.

      ✍️ “Approve” and “sign” can move your tokens

      Read what you're signing. Unlimited approvals and “permit” signatures are how drainers empty wallets.
