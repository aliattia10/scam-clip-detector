    What to do right now
      A code is a key

      WhatsApp, Instagram, your bank and your operator send a code so only you can get in. Anyone who asks you to read it back, even a friend, is using it to take an account. Codes do not reach the wrong person by mistake.

      Your friend may not be typing

      Hijacked accounts message their contacts first: a vote, a code sent by mistake, a quick Bizum until tomorrow. The name and photo are real. Call the person on their usual number before you do anything.

      Lock it before they try

      Turn on two-step verification: a PIN in WhatsApp (Settings, Account, Two-step verification) and an authenticator app on email, Instagram, Facebook and Google. Then a code alone is not enough to take your account.
