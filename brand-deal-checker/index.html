    What to do before you open, log in or pay
      Files are the trap

      Most fake sponsorships exist to make you open a file: a ZIP or RAR with a password, a “contract.pdf.exe”, a .scr or a link to download a “brief”. It installs a stealer that copies your browser sessions, so they can take your channel or page even with two-factor on.

      Brands pay you, not the other way

      A real collab never asks you to pay shipping, customs, a media kit review, an agency signing fee, a course or a verification badge, and never asks you to buy the product first and get refunded later.

      Check the sender, not the logo

      Look at the real address after the @. A Gmail, Outlook or a lookalike domain (nike-partners.co, adobe-collab.net) is not the brand. Find the brand’s official site yourself and write to the contact listed there to confirm.
